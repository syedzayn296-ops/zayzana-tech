01 / THE PILOT

One problem. One pilot.

Most businesses already have the software. The gap is the workflow between it: an enquiry, order or request comes in, and someone has to qualify it, act on it and follow up. We find the one place that is leaking time or customers, and fix that.

01

Find

Pick one repetitive process that costs you time, missed follow-ups or poor visibility.

02

Map

Walk through how it runs today: what comes in, through which channel, who handles it and where information gets re-typed.

03

Pilot

Build the smallest useful fix and run it for 14–30 days on that one process, store or team.

04

Measure

Compare before and after: time saved, follow-ups caught, errors avoided.

05

Decide

Improve it, stop, or roll it out. Your call, based on the numbers.
